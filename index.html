      How to find TOW and TOR length

      1. Diagram 1: X = Range / 1000 and Y = (OEW + Payload) / 1000. Go up from X, across from Y, and read the Brake Release Gross Weight line through the intersection, or the closest line if the point falls between lines. The label of that line is the TOW.
      2. Diagram 2: go up from the TOW to the 0 ft curve (standard day, 15 °C, 0 % gradient), then left to the take-off runway length axis. This is Lb. Convert units where needed (1,000 ft = 304.8 m).
      3. The app repeats this reading by computer from digitised copies of both curves, so TOW and Lb are approximate chart readings. Outside the plotted region the nearest chart boundary is used and the result is flagged as approximate.
